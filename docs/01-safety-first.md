# 1. Safety first

Spot weighs roughly 32 kg and moves fast enough to hurt someone. Five minutes
here saves a bad afternoon.

## Before the first power-on

- **Know where the E-Stop is.** On the controller it is the large red button on
  the touchscreen. Pressing it cuts motor power immediately — the robot will
  drop to the ground, which is intended and does not damage it.
- **Clear 2 metres around the robot** before commanding it to stand.
- **Nobody within arm's reach while it walks.** Spot's legs move faster than
  people expect, and it does not know where your feet are.
- **Watch for stairs, ledges, and glass.** Spot's obstacle avoidance handles a
  lot, but drop-offs and transparent surfaces are its weak points.

## While operating

| do | don't |
|---|---|
| Keep the controller in hand, E-Stop reachable | Leave the robot standing unattended |
| Walk behind or beside the robot | Stand in its path to "test" avoidance |
| Sit the robot before approaching it | Reach under it while powered |
| Stop if it behaves oddly, and tell us | Keep going to see if it settles |

## Water and dust

Spot is IP54 — it tolerates dust and light rain. It is **not** submersible.
Avoid puddles deep enough to reach the leg joints, and don't power-wash it.

## If something goes wrong

1. **E-Stop.** Always the first action.
2. Make the area safe.
3. Photograph anything damaged, before moving it.
4. Contact us. Please don't attempt a repair.
